Jakab Weiss

Born: 10 Jun 1873 in Edelspacher-Simand/Aradvar (Arad county), Austria-Hungary. Simand is north of Arad and is currently on the eastern border of Romania.

He was the son of David Weiss and Johanna Kohn.  His siblings were Bernhard and Rosa

Died: 22 Jan 1920 of Typhus in POW camp in Serbia during a war. 

Burial: Per his son, Robert, after the war the family got letter from priest stating that Jakab was buried in a mass grave in a Catholic cemetery in Serbia.

Married: Ernestine Blau on 11 Apr 1899 in Vienna, Austria.

Occupation:  Jakab Weiss was a very prominent and respected Cantor in Karlsbad, Austria-Hungary (it was a famous spa town and is now Karlovy Vary, Czech Republic)  He held of the position of Chief cantor known as Obercantor for the Jewish community and had a beautiful baritone voice.  He was also responsible for organizing music festivals and he brought famous musicians from around the world to Karlsbad to perform.
