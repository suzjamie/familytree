Ernestine Blau

Born: born 24 Feb 1876 in Langenzersdorf which is near Vienna, Austria.

She was the daughter of Sammuel Blau and Betti Fuchs.

Died: 12 May 1931 in Vienna, Austria 

Burial: 12 Jul 1931, Zentralfriedhof IV. Tor Group 17 Row 20 Grave 42, Vienna, Austria

Married: Jakab Weiss on 11 Apr 1899 in Vienna, Austria.

Residence: Villa Fischer Waldzeile 1011 in Karlsbad.
