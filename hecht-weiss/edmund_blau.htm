Edmund Blau

 

Born: 08 Jan 1894 in Vienna, Austria

Son of Sammuel Blau and Betti Fuchs

Married: Juliana Osman (who was born 04 Feb 1898 in Steinamanger, Szombathely, Vas, Hungary and also died about 1941 in Auschwitz Concentration Camp, Poland)

 

Died: about 1941 in Auschwitz Concentration Camp, Poland
