Robert Weiss

Born: 22 Oct 1903 in Karlsbad, Austria-Hungary*

Died: 20 Mar 1993 in Pompano Beach, Florida.

He was the son of Jakab Weiss and Ernestine Blau

Married: Rose Hecht 01 Jan 1940 in Cleveland, Oh. He married Elsa about 1983.

Immigration: Apr 1939, Flushing Holland Ship, Red Star line, From Antwerp, via South Hampton, to NYC. The first transport of Jews deported from Vienna was in October 1939.

Naturalization: 04 Dec 1944 while living at 130 Early St. Providence, RI
