Sammuel Blau and Betti Fuchs

Sammuel (SHMUEL) was born: 17 Dec 1851 in Cabaj-Cápor [Czabaj], Nyitra Megye, Hungary (now Slovakia).

Betti (ELIZABETA) was born: 1851. She had a sister Lotti who married a man named Moritz and died about 1940 in NY

 

Married: Sammuel and Betti were married about 1874.

 

Sammuel died: 29 Mar 1931 in Vienna, Austria

Betti died: 03 Jun 1933 in Vienna, Austria.

 

Sammuel's Burial: 12 Jul 1931, Zentralfriedhof gate IV. Tor Group 17 Row 20 Grave 42, Vienna, Austria

Betti's Burial: 05 Jun 1933, Vienna Zentralfriedhof gate IV. Tor Group 17 Row 20 Grave 42, Vienna, Austria

 

Children of Sammuel Blau and Betti Fuchs are:

Joseph Blau, married Therese; died about 1975 in Vancouver, Ca. Had a daughter Ilonka Blau who married Layos Berger.

Ernestine Blau

Edmund Blau (died at Auschwitz)

Possible Children of Sammuel Blau and Betti Fuchs:

Heinrich (Henrik) Blau, born 1882; died 22 Oct 1937 in Vienna, Austria. Burial: Zentralfriedhof gate IV. Tor group 17 Row 20 Grave 54

Oskar Blau, born 1886; died 17 Apr 1931; married Anna. Burial: Zentralfriedhof gate IV. Tor group 17 Row 20 Grave 54
