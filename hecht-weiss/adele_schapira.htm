Adele Schapira

 

Born: Eidel Schapira, about 1871 in Zelechów Wielki/Olszanka Mala, Poland

Died: 08 Oct 1928 in 20 Bizert Hirschvogelgasse 3, Vienna, Austria.

Burial: 09 Oct 1928, Vienna Zentralfriedhof gate IV. Tor (Row)1a Grave 15

Married: Jachel Hecht about 1891.

She was the daughter of Chaim Beer Schapira and Frimi Kneller.

Cause of Death: Gallbladder attack
