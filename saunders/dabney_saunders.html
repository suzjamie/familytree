Janet "Dabney" Saunders

Born: 05 Apr 1944 in Charleston, Kanawha Co., West Virginia

Child of: Annie Laurie "Linn" Ford and Philip Arthur Saunders

Education: Graduated from Smith College

Married: Ronald Naumchick on 05 June 1966 in North Hampton, MA in the chapel at Smith College. Wedding Invitation

Died: 06 Jan 1969 in Springfield, Hampden, Massachusetts after a car accident with a drunk truck driver.

Buried: at the Peter and Paul Russian Cemetery in Florence, Hampshire, Massachusetts
