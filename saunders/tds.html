Thomas "Davis" Saunders

Born: 21 May 1896 London, Kanawha County, West Virginia
Child of: Robert George Saunders and Annette "Nettie" Campbell Straughan
Married: Jeanne Elizabeth Roberts, 17 Mar 1920 Parkersburg, Wood, West VA
Died: 24 Apr 1982 Mauldin, WV
Military Note: USArmy #1835926, Wagoneer, Battery A, 315th Field Art.; discharges 10 June 1919 for medical reasons - slightly wounded by shell splinters at Montfaucon Oct. 6, 1918.
